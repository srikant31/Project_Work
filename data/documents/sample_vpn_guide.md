# DHTI VPN Guide

This guide explains how to connect to the institute network from home
or while travelling, using the VPN client. A VPN connection lets you
work as if you were sitting in your campus office: you can reach the
file server, the library databases, and internal web applications. The
connection is encrypted, so your work stays private even on public
Wi-Fi.

## Installing the VPN client

The VPN client is called "SecureLink". You can download it from the
software portal on the intranet, or collect a USB installer from the
IT Cell help desk during working hours. Versions are available for
Windows, macOS, and Ubuntu Linux. Install it with the default options
and restart your computer when the installer asks you to.

To complete the setup you will need:

- your employee ID, printed on your staff identity card
- your network password (the same one you use for email)
- an internet connection of at least 2 Mbps

## Connecting step by step

Open the SecureLink application. In the server address field, type
vpn.example.internal exactly as shown here. Press Tab, then choose
the profile "DHTI-Staff" from the drop-down list. Do not use the
"DHTI-Guest" profile, which is reserved for visitors.

Enter your employee ID as the username and type your network
password. Click Connect. After a few seconds you should see a green
padlock icon in the taskbar, which means the VPN tunnel is active.
You can now open the file server and internal websites normally.

## If the connection fails

Check the following before calling the help desk:

- Is your internet connection working? Open any public website first.
- Did you type the server address vpn.example.internal correctly?
- Did you choose the profile "DHTI-Staff" and enter your employee ID?
- Has your password expired? Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

If none of these fix the problem, contact the IT Cell help desk on
extension 4455 with the exact error message shown by SecureLink. Keep
a screenshot of the error if you can; it helps us diagnose the issue
much faster.

## Staying safe on the VPN

While connected, all of your internet traffic passes through the
institute network. Do not download large personal files over the VPN,
as this slows the connection for everyone. Disconnect from the VPN
when you finish your work by right-clicking the padlock icon and
choosing Disconnect. If you forget, the session ends automatically
after eight hours. Never share your employee ID or password with
anyone, and always lock your computer when you step away from it.
