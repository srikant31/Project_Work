Desk assignments

Role type	Assignment
Designated desk employee	Assigned seat, permanently reserved
Hoteling employee (hybrid)	Bookable seats, no permanent assignment
Full remote employee	No seat; day passes available on request

Booking a desk

Step	Action
1	Open the space booking tool from the intranet
2	Select the office location and date
3	Filter by preference — sit/stand, monitor, dual monitor, quiet zone
4	Select an available seat
5	Confirm. Booking appears in your calendar
6	On arrival, badge in at the neighborhood and the seat light shows your booking

Bookings open 14 days in advance and close one hour before the start of the day. Unbooked seats are available on a first-come basis but must be booked in the tool on arrival so that others can see the seat is occupied.

Booking a meeting room

Room type	Capacity	Booking rule
Focus room	1	Bookable in 30-minute blocks
Small room	4–6	Bookable in 30-minute blocks
Medium room	8–12	Bookable in 30-minute blocks
Large room	12–20	Bookable in 30-minute blocks
Training room	20–40	Bookable only by Facilities

Rooms are booked through the same space booking tool. Rooms are released automatically 10 minutes after the start time if the room is not occupied, unless the booking is marked as starting late.

Room equipment
All meeting rooms include:
Display with HDMI and USB-C input
Conference camera and microphone
Speakerphone
Whiteboard and markers
Larger rooms also include:
Dual displays
Wireless presentation
Room scheduling panel at the door
If equipment in a room is not working, submit a ticket through the Service Desk referencing FA-002 and the room name. Do not attempt to repair AV equipment yourself. Do not unplug or rearrange cabling.

Hoteling etiquette
Cancel bookings you will not use at least two hours before the start time
Do not leave personal items on a hoteling desk overnight unless you have booked it for the next day
Return chairs, monitors, and keyboards to their default position at the end of the day
Wipe down the desk at the end of the day
Do not store items in shared drawers or lockers without a Facilities-issued lock

Common issues

Issue	Resolution
Seat light shows red but seat is empty	Booking was cancelled or not confirmed; book in the tool and proceed
Room scheduling panel not responding	Use the room anyway; submit ticket to Facilities
AV equipment not detected	Check cable connection at the display first; if still not working, submit ticket
Booking not appearing in calendar	Confirm the booking completed in the tool; calendar sync can take up to 5 minutes
Someone in your booked room	Show your booking confirmation; if they do not vacate, contact Facilities

What not to do
Do not move furniture between rooms
Do not remove equipment from rooms
Do not book recurring meetings in focus rooms
Do not book rooms under another person's name
Do not use meeting rooms for storage
