Purpose
This page sets the standard for knowledge articles in the Service Desk knowledge base. It exists so that every article published meets a consistent quality bar and every agent can trust the articles they use.

What this page covers
When an article is created
What an article must contain
How articles are reviewed
When articles are retired

When to create an article
Create a knowledge article when:
A ticket is resolved and the resolution could apply to other users
A recurring issue is reported three or more times in a month
A new catalog item is added
A process or policy changes
Do not create an article for:
One-off resolutions that will not recur
Resolutions involving confidential or Restricted information (redact or do not publish)
Content that duplicates an existing article (update the existing article instead)

Article structure
Every article must have:

Section	Purpose
Title	Written as the user's question, not as the article's topic — "How do I reset my VPN password?" rather than "VPN password reset"
Applies to	Which systems, versions, and user groups the article covers
Audience	Agent-only, user-facing, or both
Summary	Two to three sentences describing the problem and the solution
Steps	Numbered, one action per step
Verification	How the agent confirms the problem is resolved
Related articles	Linked articles
Owner	Named individual, not a team
Review date	Date by which the article must be reviewed

Writing quality
Articles are written to the reading level of the audience:
User-facing articles are written in plain language, avoid jargon, and include screenshots where useful
Agent-facing articles are written in technical language, include exact system paths and tool names, and include decision points
Style rules:
Use active voice
Use imperative for steps ("Click Save", not "The user should click Save")
Do not use "simply", "just", or "obviously"
Spell out acronyms on first use
Use the current product name — do not use legacy names even if users still use them

Screenshots
Screenshots are permitted and encouraged. Screenshots must:
Show only the relevant area
Be redacted of any real user data
Be current — outdated screenshots are removed when the article is reviewed
Include alt text for accessibility

Review cycle
Articles are reviewed on the following schedule:

Article type	Review cycle
High-volume (used weekly)	Every 6 months
Standard	Annually
Low-volume	Every 2 years

Articles past their review date are flagged in the knowledge base and shown with a warning to agents.

Publishing workflow
1. Draft. Author creates the article in draft status.
2. Peer review. A second agent reviews for accuracy and clarity. Peer review is mandatory.
3. Subject matter expert review. For articles covering security, HR, or finance, an SME from the relevant team reviews.
4. Publish. The Knowledge Manager publishes the article.
Peer review typically takes 2 business days. SME review may take up to 5 business days.

Retiring articles
Articles are retired when:
The system or process they describe no longer exists
A replacement article covers the same content
The article has been flagged as inaccurate for more than 30 days
Retired articles are archived, not deleted, so that historical tickets referencing them remain intelligible.

Article metrics
The knowledge base reports:
Article views
Article usefulness rating (thumbs up / thumbs down)
Article-linked ticket resolution rate
Articles past review date
Authors see their articles' metrics monthly.
