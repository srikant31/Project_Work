1. Overview
This guide answers the questions employees most often ask about benefits enrollment and life-event changes. It is written as an FAQ because most benefits questions are situational and are best answered by matching the employee's situation to the appropriate question.
Benefits-eligible employees are those scheduled to work 30 or more hours per week. Eligibility for specific benefits may require additional criteria, which are listed in the plan documents.

2. Enrollment windows
Q: When can I enroll?
A: New hires enroll within 30 days of their start date. Existing employees enroll during the annual open enrollment window, typically in November for the following plan year.
Q: What happens if I miss the new-hire enrollment window?
A: You will not have benefits coverage until the next annual open enrollment unless you experience a qualifying life event. Contact HR Operations immediately if you believe you missed the window due to an error on ABC CORP's part.
Q: What if I don't enroll during open enrollment?
A: Your current elections carry forward for most plans. Some plans, such as flexible spending accounts, require active re-enrollment each year and do not carry forward.

3. Qualifying life events
Q: What is a qualifying life event?
A: A qualifying life event is a change in your personal circumstances that allows you to change your benefits elections outside the annual enrollment window. Common examples are listed in Section 4.
Q: How long do I have to make changes after a life event?
A: 30 days from the event date, unless the plan document specifies a different window. Events that occur on a weekend or holiday still count from the event date, not the next business day.
Q: What documentation do I need?
A: Documentation depends on the event. Marriage requires a marriage certificate. Birth or adoption requires a birth certificate or adoption decree. Loss of other coverage requires a termination letter from the other plan. HR Operations confirms the required documentation when you open the case.
Q: What if I miss the 30-day window?
A: The change cannot be processed outside the window except in cases where ABC CORP or the plan administrator made an error. Contact HR Operations and explain the situation.

4. Common life events
Q: I got married. What do I do?
A: Open a life-event case in the HRIS within 30 days. Add your spouse to the applicable plans, review beneficiary designations, and provide a marriage certificate.
Q: I had a baby. What do I do?
A: Open a life-event case within 30 days. Add the child to health, dental, and vision plans. Review life insurance beneficiary designations. Coordinate parental leave with HR-002.
Q: I got divorced. What do I do?
A: Open a life-event case within 30 days. Remove your former spouse from plans where required by the divorce decree. Review beneficiary designations. HR Operations coordinates with the plan administrator.
Q: I lost coverage through another plan. What do I do?
A: Open a life-event case within 30 days. Provide the termination letter from the other plan. If the loss of coverage is retroactive, note the effective date in the case.
Q: I moved to a new state. What do I do?
A: Update your address in the HRIS. Some plans have state-specific networks and may require a plan change. Contact HR Operations if you are unsure whether your move affects eligibility.

5. Flexible spending accounts
Q: Do I have to re-enroll in the FSA each year?
A: Yes. FSAs do not carry forward automatically.
Q: What happens to unused FSA funds?
A: Per the plan document, a limited amount carries over to the following plan year, or a grace period applies, depending on the plan year. Review the current plan document during open enrollment.
Q: Can I change my FSA election mid-year?
A: Only in connection with a qualifying life event, and only if the change is consistent with the event.

6. Beneficiary designations
Q: Do I need to update beneficiaries?
A: You should review beneficiaries at least annually and after any life event. Beneficiary designations on file with the plan administrator supersede any will or other document for these plans.
Q: How do I update beneficiaries?
A: Update designations through the plan administrator's portal. HR Operations cannot update designations on your behalf.

7. Coverage effective dates
Q: When does my coverage start?
A: New-hire coverage starts on the first day of the month following the employee's start date, unless a different effective date is stated in the plan document. Mid-year changes take effect on the first of the month following processing, unless the event requires retroactive coverage.
Q: When does coverage end?
A: Coverage ends on the last day of the month in which employment ends, unless a different rule applies. COBRA rights are communicated by the plan administrator.

8. Escalation
Benefits questions that cannot be resolved by the FAQ above should be routed to HR Operations. Benefits issues involving plan administrator errors should be documented in a case so they can be resolved with the administrator.
