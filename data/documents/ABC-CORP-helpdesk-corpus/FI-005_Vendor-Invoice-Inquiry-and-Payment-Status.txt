1. Overview
This document answers the questions most often raised by vendors and by internal requestors about invoice status and payment timing.

2. Standard payment terms
Q: What are ABC CORP's standard payment terms?
A: Net 30 from the invoice date, unless a different term is stated in the purchase order or contract. Some vendors have net 45 or net 60 terms based on negotiated contracts.
Q: When does the clock start?
A: From the invoice date, provided the invoice is complete and references a valid purchase order. Incomplete invoices are returned and the clock restarts when a compliant invoice is received.
Q: What payment methods are used?
A: ACH transfer for domestic vendors, wire transfer for international vendors, and check only where ACH is not available.

3. Invoice requirements
Q: What must an invoice include?
A: An invoice must include:
ABC CORP purchase order number
Vendor legal name and remittance address
Invoice number and date
Line-item detail matching the PO
Amount and currency
Tax where applicable
Payment terms
Q: What happens if the invoice is missing the PO number?
A: The invoice is returned. Vendors who repeatedly submit invoices without PO numbers are routed to Procurement for onboarding refresher.
Q: Where are invoices submitted?
A: To the AP invoice mailbox listed in the vendor onboarding pack. Invoices sent to individual employees are not processed until forwarded to the AP mailbox by the recipient.

4. Payment status
Q: How do I check payment status?
A: Internal requestors can check in the AP system. Vendors should contact their ABC CORP procurement contact or the AP team.
Q: How long does payment take after invoice approval?
A: Payment is made on the next scheduled payment run, which occurs weekly on Thursdays. Invoices approved by end of day Tuesday typically pay the following Thursday.
Q: Is there a way to expedite payment?
A: Expedited payment is available for invoices where ABC CORP has agreed to accelerated terms or where the vendor has flagged a hardship. Expedite requests go to the AP Manager.

5. Common reasons payment is delayed
Invoice received without a PO number
Invoice amount differs from PO
Goods receipt has not been recorded
Invoice requires additional approval based on amount
Vendor banking details not verified
Vendor insurance certificate expired
Vendor is on payment hold for compliance reasons

6. Discrepancies
Q: What if the invoice amount is different from the PO?
A: The AP team resolves discrepancies with the requestor and vendor. If the difference reflects a change in scope, a change order is required (FI-002). If the difference is an error, the vendor issues a corrected invoice.
Q: What if the goods were not received?
A: The invoice cannot be paid until goods receipt is recorded. The requestor records receipt in the procurement system.

7. Vendor holds
Vendors may be placed on payment hold for:
Expired insurance or compliance documentation
Unresolved disputes
Security findings from a vendor review (VM-001)
Non-compliance with contracting terms
Holds are removed by the team that placed them. AP notifies the vendor and the ABC CORP contact when a hold is placed.

8. Escalation
Escalate to the AP Manager if:
A payment is more than 15 days past the payment term
A vendor reports non-receipt of an ACH that AP shows as paid
A duplicate payment is suspected
Escalate to Procurement for vendor relationship issues that affect invoice processing.
